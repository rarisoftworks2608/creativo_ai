# Client guide

Welcome to Creativo AI. This is where your agency plans, creates and publishes your social
media content, and where you approve it before it goes live.

## Logging in

Use the email and temporary password from your welcome email at your agency's Creativo AI
address. Change the password after your first login (*your name → Change password*).
Forgot it? Use **Forgot password** on the login page.

## Your dashboard

- **Pending your approval:** content waiting for you.
- **At a glance:** posts published this month, what's scheduled, and your reach and
  engagement for the last 30 days.
- **Quick links** to every page your agency enabled for you. The left menu (☰ on a phone)
  shows the same pages.

## Approving content

1. Open **Approvals** (or the item from your dashboard).
2. For image posts with several versions, click **Select this version** on your favourite.
3. Click **Approve**. You can add a note for the team.
4. Not happy? Click **Request changes** and describe exactly what to change. The first time,
   the AI regenerates the content with your feedback automatically. After that, the team
   revises it by hand.
5. **History** shows every version and decision for that piece of content.

You'll get a reminder (in the app, by email if enabled, and on WhatsApp) if something waits
too long.

## After approval

- **Publishing** shows what's scheduled and what's live, with a link to each post.
- **Analytics** shows reach, engagement, your best-performing posts and platforms, and
  follower growth. Use the date filter at the top.
- **Reports** has your monthly report. View it in the browser or download it as PDF, Excel
  or CSV. It's also emailed to you each month.
- **Plan & usage** shows your plan and how much of this month's allowance is used.

## Notifications

- **Email:** *your name → My account → Email me…*
- **WhatsApp:** add your phone number with country code (+91…) under *My account* and tick
  *Send me WhatsApp updates*.

## On your phone

Everything works in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen: in the
browser menu, choose **Add to Home screen**.
